About DocMate

DocMate is a native iOS application designed to help you scan, securely store, and organize all your critical personal documents and bills in one private place.

  • Document Vault: Scan, organize, and store personal IDs, passports, warranties, and insurance documents with end-to-end security.
  • Smart Bill Tracking: Automatically track invoice due dates, utility bills, and payment deadlines.
  • Optional Gmail Integration: Connect your Gmail account to securely detect bill due dates and amounts automatically without storing email bodies.
  • Timely Notifications: Get notified before document expirations and bill due dates so you never miss a payment.

User Privacy & Security First

Your privacy is central to DocMate. When you optionally connect your Gmail account:

  • We request read-only access strictly to detect invoices and utility bills.
  • We never store, sell, or transmit full email contents.
  • Processing adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy.
