DATA & SECURITY

Privacy Policy

We believe your personal documents and finances are your business only.

1. Information We Collect

DocMate collects only information necessary to deliver its core features:

  • Account Details: Name, email address, date of birth, and gender (provided upon registration).
  • Uploaded Documents: Scans, images, and PDF records that you explicitly photograph or upload.
  • Billing Metadata: Invoice amounts, merchant names, and payment due dates extracted locally from Gmail (only when connected).
  • Notification Settings: Expiry alerts and reminder timers configured on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the DocMate service:

  • To safely encrypt, sync, and display your documents across your authenticated iOS devices.
  • To dispatch local expiry notifications and bill reminders before due dates.
  • To organize bills and warranties into structured folders.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure

DocMate's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Gmail Data & Access

If you optionally connect Gmail:

  • Read-Only Scope: We request gmail.readonly strictly to detect utility bills and receipts.
  • No Email Storage: We do not store, read, or transfer full email bodies to any server.
  • No Advertising / AI Training: Your Google data is never used for advertising, nor used to train machine learning models.
  • Revocable: Disconnect Gmail anytime via Profile → Email Sync.

4. Data Security & Storage

Tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain (encrypted by Apple's Secure Enclave). Documents and records are stored with Row-Level Security (RLS) on encrypted cloud infrastructure.

5. Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your entire account and all associated documents anytime in Profile → Account Management → Delete Account.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does DocMate keep my documents secure?

DocMate uses Apple's Secure Enclave and iOS Keychain to encrypt access tokens and sensitive credentials locally on your device. Document files are stored on secure cloud storage with strict Row-Level Security (RLS) so only you can access them.

What Gmail data does DocMate access?

DocMate requests 100% read-only access strictly to detect incoming invoice amounts, merchants, and due dates. We never read, store, or transmit your personal email bodies, and we cannot send or delete emails.

Can I disconnect Gmail or delete my account?

Yes, at any time! You can disconnect your Gmail account with a single tap in Profile → Email Sync. You can also permanently delete your account and all associated documents in Profile → Account Management.
